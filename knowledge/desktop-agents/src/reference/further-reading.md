# Further reading

## Desktop agents and current product examples

- [The Universal Tool: Why the Desktop AI Agent Is the Spreadsheet of Our
  Era](https://builder.aws.com/content/31ByviJ14ychLFYK8zJeRvHrzvl/the-universal-tool-why-the-desktop-ai-agent-is-the-spreadsheet-of-our-era)
  - A strategic argument for desktop agents as general-purpose tools across
  knowledge work.
- [ChatGPT desktop app](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app) and [Get started
  with ChatGPT Work](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/get-started-with-work) -
  Current official OpenAI entry points.
- [Claude Code getting started](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/getting-started)
  - Current official Anthropic entry point, including graphical and
  command-line forms.

Product pages change faster than the core curriculum. Review the [current
official guides](../platforms/current-official-guides.md) before teaching setup.

## Learning and curriculum design

- [Carnegie Mellon learning objectives](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/design/learningobjectives.html)
  and [principles of learning](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/principles/learning.html)
  - Alignment, prior knowledge, motivation, practice, feedback, and
  self-directed learning.
- [First Principles of Instruction](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02505024) -
  Problem-centered activation, demonstration, application, and integration.
- [How People Learn II](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/24783/how-people-learn-ii-learners-contexts-and-cultures) - Learning, transfer,
  practice, and context from the US National Academies.

## Access, AI literacy, and risk

- [CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines
  3.0](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) - Choice, relevance, multiple ways to
  access and express learning, feedback, and reflection.
- [W3C cognitive accessibility guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/),
  [Federal plain-language guidelines](https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/),
  and the [CDC Clear Communication Index](https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/index.html)
  - Clear purpose, words, steps, recovery, and audience testing.
- [What Is AI Literacy?](https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376727) and the
  [UNESCO AI competency framework for students](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students)
  - Effective use paired with critical evaluation and human-centered judgment.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
  - Contextual governance, mapping, measurement, and management of AI risk.
- [UNESCO Recommendation on Open Educational
  Resources](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-educational-resources-oer)
  - Access, adaptation, quality, and sustainable reuse of open learning
  material.
