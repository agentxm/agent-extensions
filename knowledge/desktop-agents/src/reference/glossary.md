# Glossary

**Agent:** An AI system that can pursue a goal through more than one step and
use available tools or information along the way.

**Desktop AI agent:** A person-directed AI tool that works in a computer
workspace, such as a folder, desktop app, editor, or command-line session.

**Chat:** A conversational AI exchange where the response itself is usually
the immediate result.

**Check:** A comparison with evidence outside the agent's claim, such as a
source file, calculation, test, rule, or qualified reviewer.

**Dependency:** Something a workflow needs to operate, such as a file, tool,
account, setting, policy, or another instruction set.

**Main maintained copy:** The copy that owns accepted changes and from which
other copies are made. It is sometimes called the canonical source.

**Outside effect:** An action that changes something beyond a draft workspace,
such as sending, publishing, spending, deleting, or changing an account.

**Permission:** Approval for the agent to take a particular kind of action on
a particular target.

**Project:** A saved place that groups related AI conversations, files, or
instructions. Product meanings vary.

**Source:** The material that supports a claim or supplies task information.

**Task brief:** A short assignment stating the goal, allowed sources, limits,
output, finish checks, and pause points.

**Version:** A distinct identity for a released copy, used to tell which set of
instructions or files was tested and used.

**Workflow:** A repeatable method with a trigger, inputs, steps, output, checks,
and boundaries. Some products package a workflow as a skill, rule, template,
or saved instruction.

**Workspace:** The files, tools, apps, accounts, or other environment the agent
can use for a task.
