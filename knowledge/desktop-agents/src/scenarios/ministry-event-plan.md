# Ministry event plan

**Situation:** A fictional congregation is planning a community meal. The
working folder contains facility limits, volunteer availability, a public
announcement draft, and an accessibility checklist.

**Agent fit:** The agent can combine logistics into a plan and surface missing
owners. Clergy and volunteer leaders retain pastoral, safeguarding, and
community judgment.

**Task brief:**

> Create `event-plan.md`, `volunteer-gaps.md`, and
> `announcement-draft.md` from the practice files. Include accessibility,
> food-safety, child-safeguarding, and emergency-plan checkpoints without
> inventing local rules. Mark each missing policy or owner for human review.
> Do not include pastoral notes, member needs, or private contact details. Do
> not publish or message anyone.

**Checks:** Volunteer assignments match stated availability; sensitive details
are absent; required local policies are questions, not invented answers;
announcement facts match the plan; nothing is sent.

**Measure the gain:** Count schedule conflicts and missing owners found before
the planning meeting.

**Safety boundary:** Confessional, counseling, safeguarding, giving, and member
information require strict care. Keep them outside an unapproved agent
workspace and follow denominational, legal, and organizational duties.
