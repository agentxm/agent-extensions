# Legal matter source organizer

**Situation:** A lawyer has an approved fictional matter folder containing
correspondence, dated notes, and public documents. The immediate need is an
organized chronology, not legal analysis.

**Agent fit:** An agent can locate dates, normalize a table, and link entries
back to source files. A lawyer evaluates relevance, privilege, obligations,
and strategy.

**Task brief:**

> From the fictional matter folder, create `chronology.md` with date, event,
> people named, source filename, and uncertainty. Also create
> `open-questions.md` for missing or conflicting facts. Do not make legal
> conclusions, rename evidence, search outside the folder, or quote more than
> needed to identify an event. Show the plan first.

**Checks:** Sample dates against originals; each row points to a source;
uncertain dates are labeled; no legal conclusion appears; originals and file
metadata are preserved.

**Measure the gain:** Compare the time required to locate a sampled event
before and after the chronology. Record corrections the lawyer made.

**Safety boundary:** Real legal work may involve privilege, confidentiality,
court rules, preservation duties, and client instructions. Use only approved
systems and workflows. The exercise does not establish legal suitability.
