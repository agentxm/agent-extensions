# Scenario library

Choose a scenario that resembles the **shape** of your work. Every exercise
uses fictional or public-safe information. Adapt the task, not the private
details of a real person.

## Leadership and operations

- [Executive decision brief](executive-decision-brief.md) - Combine updates
  into a source-backed choice with open questions.
- [Small-team operations review](small-team-operations-review.md) - Turn
  recurring notes and measures into an action-focused monthly review.

## Home and care

- [Household care coordination pack](household-care-coordination-pack.md) -
  Organize fictional schedules, questions, and responsibilities without making
  medical decisions.

## Professional work

- [Legal matter source organizer](legal-matter-source-organizer.md) - Build a
  fictional timeline and source index for lawyer review.
- [Patient education source pack](patient-education-source-pack.md) - Compare
  approved public health sources without giving personal medical advice.
- [Ministry event plan](ministry-event-plan.md) - Coordinate a fictional
  community event while protecting pastoral and member information.

## Learning and teaching

- [Student study pack](student-study-pack.md) - Turn allowed course notes into
  questions, explanations, and a study plan.
- [Educator lesson refresh](educator-lesson-refresh.md) - Align a fictional
  lesson with goals, activities, and a quick check for understanding.

## Software work

- [Small software change](small-software-change.md) - Inspect a fictional
  project, make one bounded code change, run its checks, and review the exact
  differences.

## More ways to adapt the pattern

The same task shapes can support a researcher comparing public sources, a
creator organizing drafts, a nonprofit preparing a grant evidence folder, a
job seeker tailoring experience notes to a posting, a field worker turning
inspection notes into a report, or a civic volunteer coordinating an event.
Start with organization and drafting; keep identity, safety, eligibility, and
professional decisions with the responsible person.
