# Household care coordination pack

**Situation:** A fictional household is coordinating school pickup, meal
support, transportation, and questions for an older relative's appointment.
Details live in separate notes and calendar exports.

**Agent fit:** The result requires combining dates and responsibilities into
several useful files. The agent organizes; family members and clinicians make
care decisions.

**Practice setup:** Use invented names and dates. Create a weekly schedule, a
list of offers from helpers, and nonmedical questions for an appointment.

**Task brief:**

> Create a one-week coordination pack with `schedule.md`, `open-needs.md`, and
> `appointment-questions.md`. Use only the practice files. Mark conflicts and
> unassigned needs. Do not infer health advice, contact anyone, or change the
> source files. Ask before resolving a conflict.

**Checks:** No invented commitments; every responsibility has an owner or is
marked open; time conflicts are visible; appointment questions are questions,
not diagnoses or instructions; nothing is sent.

**Measure the gain:** Count missed handoffs or repeated copying avoided. Ask a
second household member whether the pack makes the week easier to understand.

**Safety boundary:** Real care information can be highly sensitive. Use an
approved system and the minimum necessary details, or keep the exercise
fictional.
