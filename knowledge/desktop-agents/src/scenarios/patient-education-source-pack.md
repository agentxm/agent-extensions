# Patient education source pack

**Situation:** A clinician wants to refresh a general handout using a small set
of approved public-health sources. No patient record is involved.

**Agent fit:** The agent can compare wording, dates, and recommendations across
named sources and prepare a draft. A qualified clinician checks accuracy,
audience fit, and current guidance.

**Task brief:**

> Using only the approved public sources in this folder, create
> `source-comparison.md` and a one-page `handout-draft.md` for a general adult
> audience. Link each health claim to its source and date. List conflicts or
> unclear wording. Do not give personal advice, calculate individual risk, or
> add facts from memory. Label the output “Draft for clinician review.”

**Checks:** Links and dates are current; every health claim has support; source
differences are shown; reading level suits the intended audience; a clinician
reviews before use.

**Measure the gain:** Note time spent building the source comparison and how
many stale or unsupported statements the review catches.

**Safety boundary:** Do not use patient-identifying information in a practice
exercise. Follow privacy, clinical, organizational, and regulatory rules for
real work.
