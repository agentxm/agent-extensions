# Small-team operations review

**Situation:** A fictional small team has weekly notes, a simple measures
table, customer themes, and last month's action list.

**Agent fit:** The task repeats, spans several files, and benefits from a
consistent output and checks. Team leads still interpret causes and choose
actions.

**Task brief:**

> Create `monthly-review.md` with changes since last month, measures that need
> attention, repeated customer themes, action status, open decisions, and next
> actions. Cite the source filename beside each number and theme. Do not infer
> causes or assign owners who are not named. Show the plan first and do not
> change the source files.

**Checks:** Recalculate a sample of measures; trace themes to source notes;
separate observed change from guessed cause; carry forward unfinished actions;
mark missing owners and dates.

**Measure the gain:** Compare preparation time, corrections, and decisions
that were easier because sources and gaps were visible.

**102 extension:** Turn the task into a monthly workflow card. Test it with a
second fictional month in which one source is missing and one measure changes
format.

**Safety boundary:** Use approved accounts and remove customer, employee, and
financial details that the selected product is not authorized to process.
