# Safety and human review

Use these pages at every level. Safety depends on the information, action, and
setting—not on whether a task sounds technical.

- [Risk and review levels](risk-and-review-levels.md) - Match oversight to the
  possible harm and reversibility of the work.
- [Sensitive and high-stakes work](sensitive-and-high-stakes-work.md) - Know
  when fictional data, policy, and qualified review are required.
- [Permissions and outside effects](permissions-and-outside-effects.md) - Keep
  control over access, sending, publishing, spending, and deletion.
- [Recovery and work records](recovery-and-work-records.md) - Preserve
  originals, review changes, and leave a useful record.
