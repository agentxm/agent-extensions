# Curriculum design

Use this section to teach, evaluate, or adapt the learner material. The pages
define the curriculum contract while the 101, 102, 201, safety, and scenario
sections remain the main source content.

- [Curriculum contract](curriculum-contract.md) - Audience, purpose, scope,
  progression, and design commitments.
- [Learning outcomes and evidence](learning-outcomes-and-evidence.md) - What a
  learner should be able to do and what would demonstrate it.
- [Assessment rubrics](assessment-rubrics.md) - Shared performance criteria
  for task choice, instructions, control, checking, reuse, and management.
- [Facilitate the curriculum](facilitating-the-curriculum.md) - Entry checks,
  workshop flow, support, and debrief.
- [Adapt the curriculum](adapting-the-curriculum.md) - Create workshops,
  quickstarts, courses, or role-specific routes without copying the source of
  truth.
- [Pilot and improvement plan](pilot-and-improvement-plan.md) - Test
  comprehension, safe performance, transfer, and product independence.
- [Plain-language standard](plain-language-standard.md) - Keep material usable
  for nontechnical and neurodiverse learners.
- [Research basis](research-basis.md) - How learning, accessibility, AI
  literacy, risk, and open-resource guidance shaped the design.
