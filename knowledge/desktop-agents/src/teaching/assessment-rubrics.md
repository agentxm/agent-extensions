# Assessment rubrics

Score the work a learner produces and the decisions they make. Do not score
brand-specific button knowledge unless the course explicitly promises it.

## Shared scale

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not yet | Missing, unsafe, or cannot proceed without the facilitator making the key decision |
| With support | Completes the work after a cue or example, but does not yet transfer independently |
| Independent | Completes a new case safely, explains choices, and checks the result |
| Can guide others | Anticipates edge cases, improves the method from evidence, and helps another person without taking over |

## Performance criteria

### Task fit

- **Independent:** Chooses chat or an agent from the needed result and working
  steps; explains when the other choice would be better.
- **Not yet:** Chooses from novelty or uses an agent for high-risk work without
  a reason and safeguards.

### Task brief

- **Independent:** Names goal, sources, protected items, output, finish checks,
  and pause points with enough detail to act.
- **Not yet:** Gives a topic only, hides essential limits, or cannot tell what
  success would look like.

### Human control

- **Independent:** Reads the plan and exact permission, narrows unnecessary
  access, and keeps outside effects behind approval.
- **Not yet:** Approves broad access automatically or bypasses a control to
  continue.

### Verification

- **Independent:** Compares requirements and sampled claims with outside
  evidence, reviews actual changes, and records corrections.
- **Not yet:** Accepts the agent's self-check or polished appearance as proof.

### Reuse

- **Independent:** Separates stable instructions from changing inputs and
  tests ordinary, changed, incomplete, and unsafe cases.
- **Not yet:** Packages one successful conversation with hidden dates, names,
  permissions, or dependencies.

### Shared management

- **Independent:** Makes source, owner, origin, version, requirements, tests,
  update, rollback, and retirement inspectable.
- **Not yet:** Relies on emailed copies, memory, or an unnamed “latest” version.

## Safety gate

Mark the performance **Not yet** if the learner exposes unapproved sensitive
information, permits an unreviewed outside effect, conceals a material error,
or assigns a high-stakes decision to the agent. Provide a safe retry with
fictional inputs after a debrief.
