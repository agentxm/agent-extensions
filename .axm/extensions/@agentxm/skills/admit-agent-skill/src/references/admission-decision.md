# Agent Skill admission decision

## Decision identity

- Candidate, version, source revision, digest
- Previous accepted identity, when applicable
- Decision time and evaluator environment
- Proposed lifecycle state, consumers, cohorts, hosts, and models

## Contract and change

- Purpose, positive and negative scope
- Inputs, outputs, completion, failure behavior
- Requested and intended effective capabilities
- Dependencies, compatibility, side effects
- Compatibility class and risk delta

## Ownership and portfolio

- Responsible team, backup or escalation, acceptance evidence
- Existing, deprecated, overlapping, replacement, and composing skills
- Distinct job or consolidation rationale

## Evidence matrix

For each admission obligation, record status, evidence identity, limitation,
and responsible reviewer. Cover validation, evaluation, coexistence, audit,
provenance, enforcement, version/changelog, migration, and rollback.

## Decision

Use exactly one disposition. State scope, rationale, conditions, reviewers,
expiry, reapproval triggers, and the smallest next workflow for every defect.
