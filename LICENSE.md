# Licensing

This repository contains independently packaged AgentXM extensions. The license
for each package is the SPDX expression in its manifest. The prospective
license-selection policy and package-boundary rules are documented in
[docs/licensing.md](docs/licensing.md).

- The `agent-engineering`, `context-engineering`, `eval-engineering`, and
  `prompt-engineering` knowledge bundles are licensed under `CC-BY-4.0`.
- The `skill-engineering` knowledge bundle is licensed under
  `CC-BY-SA-4.0`.
- All other current packages are licensed under `MIT`.

Complete license texts are in [LICENSES](LICENSES). Third-party attribution is
recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
